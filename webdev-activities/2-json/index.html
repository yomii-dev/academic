About the Series

Elaina is a young, ash-haired witch who sets out to see the world for herself. Each story follows her to a new country, where she meets travelers, townsfolk, and strangers whose lives briefly cross hers. Some tales stick with her, some precious, some bittersweet. Every place brings a new story.

What makes it different?

Most fantasy stories follow one big quest. This one doesn’t. Elaina’s journey is a collection of short, self-contained stories, each set in a new country with new people. You can pick up almost any chapter and enjoy it on its own. The tone shifts from story to story: one moment funny, the next bittersweet, and often both at once.

Why read it?
  • Protagonist has little to no heroic qualities
  • Not scared of exploring darker stories
  • Episodic, self-contained stories
